Getting Started

How do I open focus. once it's installed?

focus. lives in your menu bar, not the Dock. After launching it, look for the focus icon in the top-right menu bar. Press ⌥ Space (Option + Space) from anywhere to open or close the panel, or click the menu bar icon and choose Open Focus.

Do I need to create an account?

No. focus. has no accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud. Open it and start adding sections — everything is saved on your Mac automatically.

Is focus. free?

Yes. focus. is free and open-source under the BSD-3-Clause license. No in-app purchases, no subscription, no ads.

Using focus.

How do sections and tasks work?

Each section is a column for one context — a project, a team, a domain. Add one with + section. Inside, every task moves through three states by clicking its checkbox: open → partial → done. A partial task can hold a short "what's remaining" note so you never lose where you left off.

What is the "today" panel?

The panel on the right is your focus list for the day. Right-click any task and choose to add it to today. Finished items tuck into a "done" group, and the list clears itself over from day to day so it always reflects what's left.

What's the brain-dump inbox?

The inbox is an always-first section for capturing thoughts fast. Press ⌘ I anywhere in the app to drop a new task straight into it without breaking your flow. You can turn it off in Settings → brain dump inbox.

What keyboard shortcuts are there?
  • ⌥ Space — open / close focus. from anywhere
  • ⌘ I — add a task to the inbox
  • ⌘ F — search tasks
  • ⌘ Z / ⌘ ⇧ Z — undo / redo
  • ⌘ ⇧ C — copy the whole board as text
  • Esc — close the panel (or clear search)
  • In a task: Enter for a new task, Tab to add a subtask
Can I change the look?

Yes. Open Settings (the gear icon) to switch between the Midnight Ember and Terracotta Ceramics themes, pick one of five monospace fonts, adjust the font size, and set the window size (S / M / L / fullscreen).

Data & Backups

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your Mac, in the app's private local storage. Nothing is synced or uploaded. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I back up or move my data?

Open Settings → data → export backup to save a JSON file with all your sections, tasks, and today items. On another Mac (or after reinstalling), use import backup to restore it.

How do I delete everything?

Because your data lives only on your device, deleting the app and its data removes everything permanently. You can always export a backup first if you might want it later.

Requirements

What do I need to run focus.?

macOS 13 (Ventura) or later, on Apple silicon. That's it — no other dependencies for the packaged app.

Contact & Bug Reports

Found a bug or have a question?

The best way to get help is to open an issue on GitHub — include your macOS version and steps to reproduce if it's a bug.
