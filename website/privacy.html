Privacy Policy

Last updated June 2026

Effective date: June 2026

Overview

focus. is a local-first macOS application. Every section, task, note, and setting you create is stored exclusively on your own Mac. There is no focus. server. No data is ever transmitted to, or collected by, any infrastructure operated by the developer.

We do not have accounts, sign-ins, or a backend of any kind. We cannot see your data, because it never leaves your device.

Data Storage

All of your content — sections, tasks, subtasks, "today" items, and preferences — is stored locally in the app's own storage on your Mac (the WebKit localStorage inside the application's private container). It is not synced, uploaded, or shared.

focus. operates no database, cloud service, or analytics backend. Your data is yours alone.

Network Connections

focus. makes no network requests to transmit or receive your task data. The only outbound connection the app can make is to load its typefaces:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — the interface uses monospace webfonts. Loading a font is a standard, anonymous request for a font file; it contains no personal information and no task content. If you are offline, focus. simply falls back to your system's default monospace font and works exactly the same.

Google's handling of those font requests is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. No other domains are contacted.

Analytics & Telemetry

focus. collects no analytics, crash reports, usage telemetry, advertising identifiers, or any other diagnostic data. There are no third-party tracking or advertising SDKs embedded in the app.

Your Data, Your Control

You can export a complete backup of everything at any time via Settings → data → export backup, which saves a plain JSON file to a location you choose. You can re-import it later with import backup. These files are created and read entirely on your Mac.

Data Deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, removing it is immediate and permanent. Deleting the focus. application and its associated data removes everything. You remain in full control at all times.

Children's Privacy

focus. is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone. Because no data is collected at all, no special handling is required, but we ask that parents supervise use by minors.

Source Available

focus. is source-available under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. You can inspect every line of code that runs on your Mac at github.com/pmsosa/focus. The privacy claims above are verifiable directly from the source.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published at this URL and the effective date above will be updated. Because focus. collects no data, future changes are most likely to reflect new optional features.
