Built for how people actually work

🗂️

Sections, not pipelines

One column per context — a project, a team, a domain. Not a shared "in progress" lane.

◐

Three-state tasks

open → partial → done. Partial tasks carry a short "what's remaining" note so you never lose the thread.

↳

Subtasks

Break a task into its moving parts when it needs them, and keep them tucked inside.

🧠

Brain-dump inbox

An always-first section for quick capture. Press ⌘I to drop in a thought instantly.

☀️

Today panel

Right-click any task to pull it into a focused list for the day. It clears itself as you finish.

⌨️

⌥ Space, from anywhere

A global hotkey opens and closes the panel over whatever you're doing. No app switching.

🎨

Themes & fonts

Midnight Ember or Terracotta Ceramics, five monospace typefaces, and three sizes.

🔒

Local-first, no backend

Everything is saved to your Mac. No account, no sign-up, no servers — and the source is available.
